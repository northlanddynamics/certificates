# 22: MFA and passkeys

**What to build:** Users can enroll TOTP MFA and passkeys and sign in with them. An Organization Administrator can require MFA for password users in their Organization.

**Blocked by:** 02 (Members, roles and Teams)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Users can enroll and use TOTP as a second factor.
- [ ] Users can register and sign in with passkeys.
- [ ] An Organization Administrator can require MFA; password users without MFA are made to enroll before continuing.
- [ ] Enrollment and policy changes are audited.
